Before you ask

Questions clients usually have.

Do you publish the app to the stores, or do I?

I do. I set up Play Console and App Store Connect under your accounts, handle screenshots, listings, review feedback, and rejections. You own the accounts and the app; I do the work.

Fixed price or hourly?

Either. Clear scope gets a fixed price with milestones. Ongoing or evolving work runs on a weekly rate. Both come with a weekly build you can try on your own phone.

What about the backend — do I need another developer?

No. I build the backend too: Postgres on Supabase or a Kotlin/Spring Boot service, plus an admin panel for your team. If you already have an API, I'll integrate with it.

Can you take over an existing app?

Yes. I'll audit the codebase first, tell you honestly what's salvageable, and give you a plan — whether that's stabilizing what's there or a rebuild.

Time zone and communication?

I'm in Bangladesh (UTC+6) and have worked remotely with teams in the USA, Germany, the UAE, Ireland, and India. WhatsApp, email, Slack, or Meet — whatever your team already uses.
